# Government and Public Authority Data Request Policy

**Owner:** [name, role]
**Effective date:** [date adopted]
**Review cycle:** Annually, or on any material change to applicable law.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs how EasilyPromote responds when a public authority — law enforcement, a regulator, a court, a tax authority, or any government body — requests personal data about our users.

It applies to all personal data we hold, including:

- Creator and brand account data (name, email, phone, payout details)
- Platform Data received from Meta and TikTok (platform user IDs, usernames, profile pictures, access tokens)
- Campaign, submission, and payment records

It applies to every employee, contractor, and officer of the company. No one may disclose user data to a public authority except through this policy.

## 2. Intake

All requests must be routed immediately to the policy owner named above. No other person may respond to, acknowledge the substance of, or act on such a request.

A request received by any other route — a direct approach to an engineer, a phone call, a message to a support inbox — must be forwarded unanswered to the policy owner on the day it is received.

## 3. Required review of legality

Before any data is disclosed, the policy owner must confirm and record each of the following:

1. **Authority.** The request comes from a body with jurisdiction over EasilyPromote or the data subject.
2. **Legal basis.** The request cites a specific legal instrument — warrant, court order, statutory notice, or equivalent — and that instrument is valid on its face.
3. **Form.** The request is in writing, signed, and issued by an identifiable officer. Verbal or informal requests are refused pending written service.
4. **Specificity.** The request identifies particular accounts, records, or a defined time period. Blanket or exploratory requests are not lawful bases for disclosure.
5. **Cross-border effect.** Where the data is stored in a different jurisdiction from the requesting authority, the transfer must have a lawful basis before disclosure. Our production database is hosted in France; EU data protection law applies to that data.

Where any of these cannot be confirmed, the request is treated as unlawful under section 4 until resolved.

## 4. Challenging unlawful requests

Where the policy owner concludes that a request is unlawful, overbroad, defective, or unclear, EasilyPromote will:

- Decline to produce data pending resolution.
- Seek external legal advice before responding substantively.
- Where available, formally object, seek narrowing, or move to quash through the appropriate legal channel.
- Where permitted by law, notify the affected user before disclosure, so that they may seek their own remedy. Where a valid non-disclosure order prohibits notice, we will seek to notify once that order lapses.

Cost or inconvenience is never a reason to comply with a request believed to be unlawful.

## 5. Data minimisation

Where disclosure is lawful and required, EasilyPromote discloses the minimum necessary:

- Only the specific records named in the request. We do not volunteer adjacent data because it is convenient to export.
- Only the fields required. Where a request seeks identity confirmation, we do not supply full account history.
- Access tokens and credentials are never disclosed. Platform Data obtained from Meta or TikTok is disclosed only where it falls squarely within the scope of a valid order.
- Where a request can be satisfied with aggregated or redacted data, that form is offered first.

The policy owner records what was withheld, and why, alongside what was produced.

## 6. Documentation

A record is kept for every request received, whether or not data was disclosed. Each entry contains:

- Date received, requesting authority, and the individual officer named.
- The legal instrument relied on, and a copy of the request as served.
- The people involved in the review, internal and external.
- The legal reasoning for the decision to disclose, narrow, or refuse.
- Exactly what data was produced, in what form, on what date, and what was withheld.
- Whether the affected user was notified, and if not, the legal basis for withholding notice.

Records are retained for a minimum of five years and are available for audit.

## 7. Transparency

Subject to legal constraints, EasilyPromote intends to report periodically on the number and type of public authority requests received and our responses to them.
