# EasilyPromote

A marketplace where brands pay creators to make content and drive results on social media. This glossary covers campaigns, creators, approvals and money.

## Language

### People

**Brand**:
A company that funds campaigns. Stored as a user with the business role.
_Avoid_: Business, client, advertiser

**Creator**:
A person who joins campaigns and makes content on their own social accounts.
_Avoid_: Influencer, promoter, user

**Admin**:
EasilyPromote staff who review, set rewards, run payouts and resolve disputes.
_Avoid_: Moderator, operator

### Campaigns

**Campaign**:
A funded brief from a brand that creators join to earn money.
_Avoid_: Job, gig, promotion

**Campaign Model**:
What a campaign pays for: a **Content Campaign** pays per approved deliverable; a **Performance Campaign** pays per verified result.
_Avoid_: Campaign type, UGC campaign, distribution campaign

**Objective**:
The outcome the brand wants: content, views, engagement, downloads, sign-ups, leads, sales or other.
_Avoid_: Goal, KPI

**Deliverable**:
One piece of content a content campaign pays for once approved, such as one video.
_Avoid_: Asset, post, output

**Brief**:
Everything a creator needs to make the content: instructions, do's and don'ts, hashtags, sound, references, tone, key messages and product info.
_Avoid_: Creative, instructions, requirements

**Content Destination**:
Where approved content ends up: the creator's page, the brand's page, or both.
_Avoid_: Channel, distribution

**Usage Rights**:
The brand's permission to use a creator's content on its own channels, granted when content goes to the brand's page.
_Avoid_: Licence, IP

### Access and approval

**Creator Access**:
How creators get into a campaign: **Open Call** (any eligible creator joins instantly) or **Application Required** (the brand picks from applicants).
_Avoid_: Campaign visibility, private campaign

**Placement**:
A creator's reserved place in a campaign, with what they're expected to deliver and earn.
_Avoid_: Slot (code name only), seat, spot

**Join**:
Taking a placement in an Open Call campaign.
_Avoid_: Claim, enrol, accept

**Application**:
A creator's request to take part in an Application Required campaign, carrying a snapshot of their profile at that moment.
_Avoid_: Pitch, proposal, bid

**Creator Approval**:
The brand's decision that a creator may take part. Applies only to Application Required campaigns.
_Avoid_: Acceptance, selection (as a status)

**Content Approval**:
The brand's decision that submitted content meets the brief. Separate from Creator Approval; neither ever decides the other.
_Avoid_: Review (on its own), sign-off

**Submission**:
Content a creator sends for Content Approval, with its history of changes and posting.
_Avoid_: Draft, entry, upload

### Matching

**Audience Targeting**:
Who the brand wants to reach: audience location, age, gender, interests and platform.
_Avoid_: Creator targeting, demographics filter

**Audience Location**:
Where a creator's followers are, as a share of their audience. Targeting uses this, never where the creator lives.
_Avoid_: Creator location, region

**Creator Eligibility**:
The requirements a creator must meet to join or apply: followers, engagement, categories, verification, rank, badges.
_Avoid_: Requirements, qualifications

**Match Score**:
How well a creator's audience fits a campaign's audience targeting; orders recommendations and applicants.
_Avoid_: Fit, relevance

**Verified Creator**:
A creator with a connected social account whose identity admin has checked.
_Avoid_: Approved creator, KYC'd

**Rank**:
A creator's standing from rank 1 to elite, earned from verified results; some placements require a minimum rank.
_Avoid_: Level, tier

**Badge**:
An earned mark on a creator's profile: Top Creator, High Performer, Reliable Creator, Campaign Pro.
_Avoid_: Award, achievement

### Money

**Pay Shape**:
How a creator's earnings are structured: **Fixed** (a set amount per approved deliverable), **Performance** (per verified result) or **Hybrid** (a base plus a performance bonus).
_Avoid_: Compensation model, payment type

**Rate Authority**:
Who sets what a creator earns per unit: the brand for content, admin for sign-ups and other referral actions, EasilyPromote's price table for views.
_Avoid_: Pricing owner

**Referral Budget**:
Money a brand pays for sign-ups or other actions, from which admin-set rewards are paid.
_Avoid_: Action budget, conversion budget

**Reward**:
What a creator earns for one verified conversion, set by admin.
_Avoid_: Bounty, commission, CPA

**Conversion**:
One verified action (install, sign-up, lead, purchase, deposit) credited to a creator's referral code, reported by the brand's server.
_Avoid_: Referral, lead, event

**Referral Code**:
The code identifying one creator in one campaign, used to credit conversions.
_Avoid_: Promo code, tracking link

**Platform Fee**:
EasilyPromote's share of what a brand pays for a campaign.
_Avoid_: Commission, margin, service charge

**Creator Pool**:
The part of a campaign's money reserved for paying creators.
_Avoid_: Escrow, payout budget

**Hold**:
The 7 days earnings wait before they can be withdrawn.
_Avoid_: Lock, pending period

**Withdrawal**:
A creator's request to be paid a campaign's available earnings, sent in the weekly Friday payout.
_Avoid_: Cash-out, payout request

**Refund**:
Money returned to a brand from a campaign's unused budget.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback
