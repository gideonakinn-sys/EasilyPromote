# 10: Hybrid pay — base plus performance bonus

**What to build:** A brand can offer a base fee per approved deliverable plus a performance bonus. Creators see it on the marketplace under a Hybrid tab, get the base when their content is accepted and the bonus as results come in.

**Blocked by:** 03, 04, 09

**Milestone:** M7b · after launch · 18 Nov – 18 Dec

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Wizard offers Hybrid for content campaigns: brand sets base per deliverable and funds a bonus pool; bonus metric and cap per D4
- [ ] Checkout charges base budget + bonus pool + platform fee from the shared calculator
- [ ] Marketplace gains the Hybrid tab; cards read like `₦5,000 + bonus`
- [ ] Base is paid on the fixed-pay trigger; the bonus is reserved from the bonus pool as views or conversions are verified, up to the per-creator cap
- [ ] Wallet shows base and bonus separately
- [ ] Reconciliation passes with Hybrid campaigns included
